BOS / CHoCH markers
Consecutive closes back through the level before a break is called failed. 1 is the exact mirror of the break rule — and it is wrong more often than it is right. Measured on 70,215 real 5m bars across 15 names (2,732 breaks): at 1, 63% of "failed" verdicts saw price close back through the level the break's own way within the hour, and overall verdict accuracy was 49.6%. At 3 it is 66.2%, and the same knee shows up on 10m, 1H, 4H and 1D. The cost is a slower call (median 7 bars instead of 2). 3 is the default for that reason; lower it only if you want the twitchier read back.
